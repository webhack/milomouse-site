# Milo Mouse — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26 October 2026_

Milo Mouse is a single-player game made by Oleksandr Chumak (Web Help Agency).

**We do not collect any data.** The app has no account system, no analytics, no
advertising, no third-party SDKs and makes no network requests.

**What stays on your device.** The game saves your progress (stars per stage,
best times, chosen difficulty, sound on/off and whether you have seen the
intro story) in a small settings file inside the app's own storage on your
device. It never leaves the device and is removed when you delete the app.

**Children.** The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal
information from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If a future version ever changes what the game stores or sends, this
page will be updated first and the App Store privacy label with it.

**Contact.** Questions about this policy: oleksandr@webhelpagency.com
